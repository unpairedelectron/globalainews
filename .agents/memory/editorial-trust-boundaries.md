---
name: Editorial trust boundaries
description: Public attribution and corrections/contact promises require publication approval.
---

- Public bylines and profile pages may identify only contributors verified for publication; otherwise use the Global AI News Editorial Desk identity.
- Corrections and contact pages may describe only approved, operating routes. If none exists, say so, keep the pages noindex, and do not present newsletter signup as a reporting channel.

**Why:** Unverified names and unapproved routes would make public claims about identities or services without evidence.

**How to apply:** Enforce this at the public API, article/research/AMP rendering, trust-page copy, and SEO indexability. Do not invent biographies, credentials, addresses, forms, service levels, or response promises.
