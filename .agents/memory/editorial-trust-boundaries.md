---
name: Editorial trust boundaries
description: Public attribution and corrections/contact promises require publication approval.
---

- Public bylines and profile pages may identify only contributors verified for publication; otherwise use the Global AI News Editorial Desk identity.
- Trust and policy pages should remain indexable when they offer factual, useful disclosures, even when an approved contact or corrections route is not available. State the limitation plainly; do not present newsletter signup as a reporting channel.
- Keep utility/search routes and unverified individual profile routes non-indexable according to their route metadata.

**Why:** Unverified names and unapproved routes would make public claims about identities or services without evidence; noindexing otherwise useful trust disclosures would hide the limits readers need to understand.

**How to apply:** Enforce this at the public API, article/research/AMP rendering, trust-page copy, and SEO indexability. Do not invent biographies, credentials, addresses, forms, service levels, or response promises. A clearly labeled, evidence-bounded privacy or terms placeholder is preferable to invented legal language while publisher approval is pending.
