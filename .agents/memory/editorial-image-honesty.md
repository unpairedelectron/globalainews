---
name: Editorial image honesty
description: Editorial labeling and delivery expectations for generated story artwork in Global AI News.
---

Story imagery used as conceptual artwork must be explicitly labeled as an illustration and must not imply that it documents the reported real event. Prefer locally hosted, optimized assets and a local fallback over external hotlinks.

**Why:** generated visuals are useful for coverage but can be mistaken for documentary evidence in a news product.

**How to apply:** retain a visible “Editorial illustration” caption where artwork appears, use descriptive alt text, and preserve the local fallback when adding story artwork.
