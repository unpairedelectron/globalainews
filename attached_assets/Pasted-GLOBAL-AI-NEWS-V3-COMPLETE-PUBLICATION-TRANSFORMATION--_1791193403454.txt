GLOBAL AI NEWS V3 — COMPLETE PUBLICATION TRANSFORMATION
========================================================

MISSION

Transform the currently published Global AI News site into a genuinely
premium AI publication and research intelligence platform.

This is NOT another cosmetic redesign.

The objective is to make the production site materially better in:

- editorial quality
- factual depth
- source transparency
- information density
- visual storytelling
- search usefulness
- authority
- reader experience
- homepage freshness
- research discoverability
- data presentation
- internal discovery
- mobile experience
- performance
- trust
- overall perceived quality

The final product should feel like a serious publication/research
organization, not an AI-generated article site.

Improve the actual publication.

========================================================
NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES
========================================================

1. QUALITY OVER ARTICLE COUNT

70 published articles do not matter if 30 of them are mediocre.

Every published article must justify its existence.

You may:

- rewrite
- expand
- consolidate
- differentiate
- replace
- unpublish
- noindex

content when genuinely necessary.

Do not preserve weak content to satisfy an arbitrary number.

2. NO FABRICATION

Never invent:

- facts
- statistics
- citations
- URLs
- quotes
- authors
- credentials
- dates
- research findings
- benchmarks
- datasets
- company claims
- GPU availability

When evidence does not exist:

say so.

3. SOURCE FIRST

Use the existing source registry and source captures wherever applicable.

Every important factual or quantitative claim must be traceable.

Prefer:

primary government sources
official organizations
research papers
first-party company documentation
authoritative datasets
high-quality journalism

Do not use a publisher homepage when the exact source document is known.

4. FACT ≠ ANALYSIS

Clearly distinguish:

reported fact
source-backed synthesis
original analysis
editorial interpretation

Never make speculation look factual.

5. DO NOT WRITE AI-SLOP

Avoid:

generic introductions
obvious summaries
repetitive conclusions
meaningless adjectives
fake sophistication
generic "AI is transforming..." openings
filler paragraphs
repeated templates
keyword stuffing
empty statistics
manufactured certainty
