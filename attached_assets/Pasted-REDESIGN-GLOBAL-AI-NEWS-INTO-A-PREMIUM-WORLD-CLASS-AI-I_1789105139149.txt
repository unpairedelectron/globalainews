REDESIGN GLOBAL AI NEWS INTO A PREMIUM, WORLD-CLASS AI INTELLIGENCE PUBLICATION.

Reference:
https://global-ai-media.replit.app/

Existing product:
https://globalainews.in/

Do NOT create another generic AI-news SaaS template.

The current implementation has the visual characteristics of an AI-generated website: excessive gradients, rounded cards, generic dashboard patterns, weak editorial hierarchy, and insufficient brand authority.

The new design must feel like a serious global media institution.

CORE POSITIONING

GLOBAL AI NEWS
Intelligence for the AI economy.

The product is not simply an AI news aggregator.

It is an intelligence platform that transforms AI news into:
- stories
- signals
- context
- evidence
- timelines
- company intelligence
- model intelligence
- industry intelligence
- policy intelligence

DESIGN DIRECTION

Use:
- premium editorial design
- strong typography
- warm white / ivory backgrounds
- near-black typography
- one restrained electric blue accent
- editorial serif for major headlines
- modern grotesk for UI and metadata
- generous whitespace
- thin rules
- asymmetrical editorial grids
- subtle motion
- high-quality image treatment
- restrained data visualization
- excellent hierarchy

Avoid:
- purple/blue gradients
- glowing blobs
- glassmorphism
- excessive rounded cards
- excessive pills
- generic AI illustrations
- futuristic decorative graphics
- dashboard aesthetics
- excessive icons
- unnecessary animations
- visual clutter

The site must feel expensive, intelligent, calm and authoritative.

HOMEPAGE

Create the following hierarchy:

1. GLOBAL AI NEWS masthead
2. Primary navigation:
Brief / Signals / Research / Industry / Models / Policy / India / Essays
3. Date + edition indicator
4. Large editorial statement:
"The AI industry is moving fast. We make sense of it."
5. Lead story
6. Today's Signals
7. Top stories
8. What Changed Today timeline
9. AI Industry Map
10. India AI Index
11. Deep Reads / Essays
12. Daily Brief newsletter CTA
13. Institutional footer

Do NOT make the homepage a grid of equal-sized cards.

Create clear editorial hierarchy with one dominant story and secondary stories.
