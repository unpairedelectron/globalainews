======================================================
CONTENT SEGMENTS
======================================================

SEGMENT 1 — BRIEF
10 pieces

Purpose:
Fast, high-quality reporting on important developments.

Each Brief should answer:

What happened?
Who is involved?
What changed?
What is confirmed?
Why does it matter?

Do not turn a Brief into a shallow press-release rewrite.

Minimum expectation:

- strong opening
- clear factual summary
- important context
- primary source whenever available
- exact dates
- relevant entities
- useful "why it matters"
- related deeper coverage

======================================================
SEGMENT 2 — ANALYSIS
10 pieces

Purpose:
Explain implications and consequences beyond the headline.

Each Analysis must contain:

- factual foundation
- evidence
- competing considerations where relevant
- original synthesis
- implications
- what could happen next
- clearly separated analysis/opinion

Do not present speculation as fact.

SEGMENT 3 — EXPLAINER

- economics

- workflows

EDITORIAL QUALITY REVIEW
======================================================

Before publishing each article, automatically review:

1. Is the premise genuinely useful?
2. Is this materially different from competitor coverage?
3. Are claims source-backed?
4. Are sources direct and relevant?
5. Is the search intent satisfied?
6. Is the headline accurate and compelling?
7. Is the article logically structured?
8. Is there unnecessary repetition?
9. Are numbers correctly contextualized?
10. Are facts separated from analysis?
11. Are limitations disclosed?
12. Are internal links meaningful?
13. Is the article connected to the authority graph?
14. Is the page technically indexable?
15. Does first-response HTML contain the essential content?
16. Does mobile presentation work?
17. Does AMP work?
18. Would an expert consider the article credible?
19. Would a reader find it genuinely useful?
20. Does it deserve to rank?

If the answer to the final question is no:

DO NOT PUBLISH.

Rewrite or replace it.
