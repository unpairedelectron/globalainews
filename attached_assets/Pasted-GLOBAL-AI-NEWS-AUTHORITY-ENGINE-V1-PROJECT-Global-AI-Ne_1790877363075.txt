==================================================

Before implementing the Authority Engine, perform a lightweight gate check.

DO NOT BEGIN THE GROWTH ARCHITECTURE if any of these remain unresolved:

1. private editorial APIs are publicly exposing editorial/workflow data
2. publication lifecycle state cannot be reconciled
3. published vs scheduled content cannot be reliably distinguished
4. production article first-response metadata is not correct
5. major public routes are broken
6. sitemap contains incorrect URLs
7. public trust pages remain unintentionally broken
8. newly published content cannot reliably become public
9. article authors are incorrect
10. article source attribution is incorrect

Performance does NOT need to be perfect before planning, but severe regressions must be understood.

If a prerequisite is unresolved:

STOP.

Report the blocker.

Do not create dozens of new pages on top of an unreliable content system.
