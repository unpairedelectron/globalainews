========================================================
EDITORIAL TYPE STANDARDS
========================================================

BRIEF

Fast factual reporting.

Structure:

What happened
What is confirmed
Context
Why it matters

Target roughly 600–1,000 words where the subject warrants it.

ANALYSIS

Must contain actual interpretation beyond reporting.

Evidence
Argument
Counter-considerations
Implications
What changes next

Target roughly 1,200–2,000 where warranted.

EXPLAINER

Should answer a real question completely.

Concept
How it works
Examples
Implications
Misconceptions

Target roughly 1,500–2,500 where warranted.

RESEARCH

Must contain:

research question
methodology
evidence/data
findings
limitations
sources

Do not call article summaries research.

REPORT

Must act as a durable reference asset.

Include where relevant:

executive summary
current state
historical context
data
findings
methodology
limitations
sources
update history

COMPARISON

Must help a decision.

Compare actual dimensions.

Do not invent benchmarks.

ESSAY

Must have a distinctive argument supported by evidence.

Do not write generic "AI is changing everything" essays.

========================================================
CONTENT WRITING STANDARD
========================================================

Weak:

"Companies are investing heavily in AI infrastructure."

Preferred:

Name the companies.

Name the locations.

Name the programs/facilities.

Give dates.

Give documented figures.

Explain what those figures establish.

State what they do NOT establish.

Source it.

Turn abstract statements into concrete reporting whenever evidence permits.
