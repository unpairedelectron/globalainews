# 5. P0 — LEGACY CONTENT / INDEX CLEANUP SYSTEM

Audit every existing article and route.

Classify URLs into:

### KEEP + INDEX

Current, relevant, useful, sufficiently substantive content.

### KEEP + NOINDEX

Useful to users but not useful as standalone search landing pages.

Examples may include certain filtered listings, internal search pages, or low-value archives.

### REDIRECT

Old URL with a clear equivalent current canonical URL.

### REMOVE / 410

Irrelevant, obsolete, useless legacy content where no meaningful replacement exists.

### REPAIR

Content that should remain but currently has a broken URL, metadata problem, or missing structure.

Do not blindly redirect everything to the homepage.

Do not keep irrelevant historical content merely because it already exists.

Do not create fake replacement pages purely for SEO.

Create an internal migration/audit report.
