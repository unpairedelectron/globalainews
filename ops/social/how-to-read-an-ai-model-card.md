## LinkedIn
Model cards were proposed in 2018 to stop models being used in contexts they don't suit. Eight years on, most releases have one, and most readers skip to the benchmark table.

Those scores are almost always the developer's own measurements. The sections with real operational consequence are the licence, the out-of-scope uses, and the limitations, and the quality gap between a card that states its limits plainly and one that lists only wins is large.

Our guide walks through all eight standard sections and what a missing one tells you.
