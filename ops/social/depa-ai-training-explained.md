## LinkedIn
"Under DEPA, the model travels to the data, rather than the data travelling to the model." That's how India's Principal Scientific Adviser describes MeitY's new framework for training AI on sensitive institutional data.

Hospitals, banks and public agencies keep their data inside secure environments. Startups and researchers train there under electronic contracts and differential privacy, and models must pass bias, security and explainability checks before deployment.

Our explainer covers how it works, how it fits with the DPDP Act, and what's still undecided, starting with who oversees it.
