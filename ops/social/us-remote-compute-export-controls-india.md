## LinkedIn (≤120 words)

The most consequential line in Washington's new export rule isn't about a country. It's about a customer.

A data centre anywhere now needs a US licence if it supplies more than 12,000,000 "aggregate TPP" of compute to firms headquartered in Macau or an arms-embargoed country. Location is explicitly irrelevant, and the ceiling aggregates across digitally linked sites.

India is not a target: Country Group A:5, and absent from Country Group D entirely.

What does reach Indian operators is diligence. A data centre that can't show who ultimately owns its customers becomes a "red flag" that presumptively gives its chip supplier knowledge of a prohibited end use.

The same rule also deleted the export control on AI model weights.
