## Telegram

**You now apply for AI cyber capability. You don't buy it.**

Both Anthropic and OpenAI ship general models that block most security work, and restore it only after vetting.

**Anthropic — Cyber Verification Program, three tiers:**
• Defense Access — SOC, incident response, malware RE. Individuals with a track record of reported vulns can qualify. Reply in "a few days".
• Red Team Access — authorised pen testing. **Organisations only.** A few weeks.
• Specialized Access — fewest blocks, for orgs authorised to test power grids, telecom, interbank infrastructure. Reviewed "in collaboration with the US government".

**OpenAI — Daybreak, two tiers:** Blue (defensive) and Red (authorised red teaming). Accepts "an individual or an organization" for both.

**The honest number:** on CyScenarioBench, every task was blocked without access; 46 of 50 blocked at Defense tier; at Red Team tier no blocks and 34 of 50 completed — matching the 67.6% unguarded rate.

**Strings:** data retention required for misuse monitoring; upper tiers need Enterprise or API plans.

None of this applies to a model whose weights you hold.
