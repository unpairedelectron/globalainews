## LinkedIn
Buried in Microsoft's Ignite announcements: its new Decision-1.1 model, already routing requests inside Copilot, is "built on a new base, Gemma 4 26B A4B". That's Google's open-weight family.

In the same keynote, Satya Nadella announced MAI Licensed Weights, so trusted customers can get access to Microsoft's own model weights.

A company selling frontier model access is running production systems on a competitor's open weights and preparing to distribute its own. Open weights aren't a hobbyist concern any more. But Gemma ships under Google's own terms, not a standard open-source licence, so the licence is what to read.
