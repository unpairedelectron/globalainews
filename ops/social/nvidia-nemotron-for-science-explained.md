## LinkedIn
NVIDIA has released Nemotron for Science: an open vision-language model, an open chemistry dataset of nearly 50,000 questions, and post-training recipes for building research agents.

For labs that cannot send confidential data to an external API, the deployment model matters more than any benchmark. The weights are downloadable and NVIDIA says the model was optimised to run on a 128GB desktop system.

The reported gains are striking, but nearly all come from the companies that did the post-training. The clearest result is KTH's: an agent that repaired 81% of broken simulation setups overnight, with every fix verified independently by the simulation software itself.
