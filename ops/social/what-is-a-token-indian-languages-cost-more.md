## LinkedIn
Research has found that the same text, translated into different languages, can differ in token count by up to 15 times.

Since AI pricing, context windows and speed are all measured in tokens, that gap is a real cost for anyone building in Hindi, Tamil or Bengali. The cause is tokenizer vocabularies built largely from English text.

Indian labs are responding: ZenteiQ's Dhīr models ship a 131,000-entry tokenizer for English and ten Indian languages, and Soket lists token-efficient Indic vocabularies as a research direction.
