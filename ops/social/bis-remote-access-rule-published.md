## LinkedIn (≤120 words)

Commerce published its remote-access chip rule on 19 November and it took effect the same day.

The control follows the customer rather than the building: a licence is required for advanced chips destined for a data centre "wherever located" that supplies more than 12,000,000 "aggregate TPP" to companies headquartered in Macau or an arms-embargoed country. Operators already serving such customers have 90 days to get a licence.

Two things worth noting. The rule also deletes the AI Diffusion Rule's export control on model weights outright. And BIS says the rule was "written by and at the direction of the Department of Treasury and the National Security Council".

Comments close 19 January 2027.
