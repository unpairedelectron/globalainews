| Initial requested capacity is up to 10,000 AICU, with clusters of 4,000, 1,000, 512 or a Stage-2 size; C-DAC may scale beyond 10,000 according to demand. | [V] | C-DAC [1], Tier 1 | Request plus expansion permission, not deployed or guaranteed capacity. |
| Stage 1 is technical qualification; Stage 2 is procurement through GeM. Stage-1 qualification does not guarantee work. | [V] | C-DAC [1], Tier 1 | Preserve the separation between empanelment, award, provisioning and use. |
| The requested service period is five years. | [V] | C-DAC [1], Tier 1 | A service-period term is not proof that all requested units are contracted. |
