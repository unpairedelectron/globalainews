# Fact log — EU AI Act application dates

Prepared 31 October 2026. `[V]` denotes a claim checked against official EU text or guidance; limitations are recorded where a summary is not legally binding.

| Claim used in the draft | Tag | Source and tier | Check / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 entered into force on 27 July 2026. | [V] | European Commission [2], Tier 1 | Commission dates the event; Regulation 2026/1744 is the controlling amending text [1]. |
| Article 113 now contains separate dates for Article 5, Article 50(2), Annex III and Annex I. | [V] | Consolidated Article 113 [3], Tier 1 | Keep provision and category attached to each date. |
| Specified additions to Article 5(1), points (ba)/(bb), and paragraphs (1a)/(1b) apply from 2 December 2026. | [V] | Article 113 [3], Article 5 [5], Tier 1 | Article 5 scope and exceptions require case-specific reading; the article avoids classifying products. |
| Article 50(2) marking obligation concerns machine-readable marking and detectability of generated or manipulated content. | [V] | Article 50 [4], Tier 1 | Applies to providers of AI systems, including GPAI systems, in the statutory scope. |
| Providers of systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 receive until 2 December 2026 for Article 50(2). | [V] | Article 113 [3], Tier 1 | This is not an extension of every Article 50 duty. |
| Article 6(2)/Annex III high-risk obligations under Chapter III Sections 1–3 apply from 2 December 2027. | [V] | Article 113 [3], Article 6 [6], Annex III [8], Tier 1 | Classification depends on statutory tests and use. |
| Relevant Article 6(1)/Annex I systems follow 2 August 2028. | [V] | Article 113 [3], Article 6 [6], Annex I [7], Tier 1 | Limited to the product and conformity-assessment conditions in Article 6(1). |
| The Commission timeline describes the AI Act as a staged rollout, not a single 2028 start. | [V] | Commission AI Act page [9], Tier 1 | Explanatory summary; Official Journal text controls. |
| No particular product is classified as prohibited, high-risk or exempt in this draft. | [V] | Editorial scope | The article is a date explainer, not legal advice or product-specific classification. |
