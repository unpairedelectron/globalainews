# Fact log — workforce announcements and AI causation

Prepared 31 October 2026. `[V]` means checked in a primary or official source; `[S]` marks secondary reporting or general research context.

| Claim used in the draft | Tag | Source and tier | Check / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Microsoft announced a Voluntary Exit Offer on 26 October for eligible employees in participating organizations. | [V] | Microsoft [1], Tier 1 | Company statement confirms the offer, not acceptance totals or net workforce change. |
| Reuters reported an estimated 60,000 potentially eligible Microsoft employees. | [S] | Reuters via CBC [3], Tier 2 | Potential eligibility is not departures; attributed to reporting, not presented as independent count. |
| Dutch government aims to reduce central-government staffing by an estimated 25,000 FTE over several years. | [V] | Government of the Netherlands [2], Tier 1 | A target measured in FTE, not a completed count of individual job losses. |
| Dutch target is tied to savings goals; police and defence are among exceptions stated. | [V] | Government of the Netherlands [2], Tier 1 | Preserve the announcement’s time horizon and stated exclusions. |
| Dutch government separately says AI productivity goals and savings plans are due in Q1 2027. | [V] | Government of the Netherlands [2], Tier 1 | The primary statement does not say AI causes the 25,000-FTE target. |
| Secondary coverage associates the Microsoft offer with AI spending and describes the Dutch plan as AI-led. | [S] | Reuters/CBC [3], AP [4], NYT [5], Bloomberg [6], Tier 2 | Use only to describe media framing; official statements do not establish specific AI-caused totals. |
| ILO exposure research measures task/occupational exposure, not completed job losses in these organizations. | [V] | ILO [7], Tier 1 research | General context; not a causal assessment of either announcement. |
| OECD automation-risk findings are broad labor-market context, not a forecast for the two cases. | [V] | OECD [8], Tier 1 research | Do not recast multi-technology automation estimates as AI-only or employer-specific. |
| Neither primary statement attributes a defined number of job losses to AI. | [V] | Microsoft [1] and Dutch Government [2], Tier 1 | This is a bounded reading of the checked texts, not a claim about all internal decision-making. |
| Article is AI-assisted and unpublished. | [V] | Editorial process | Disclosed in methodology; no public route or release record. |
