| # | Brief | Primary-source evidence checked | Current decision |

| 1 | Anthropic S-1 governance | [SEC S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm), filed 26 October 2026, confirms governance and financial figures. It reports $16.3B H1 2026 revenue versus $1.2B H1 2025 and $4.6B FY2025 revenue; $80.7B H1 2026 net loss, including a $72.4B change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs; and top-five customers at approximately 25% of revenue for the nine months ended 30 September 2026. The filing attributes most of the fair-value change to increased value of convertible promissory notes as enterprise valuation increased. | Use the filing’s named periods and explanation. Do not turn accounting fair-value movements into cash spending or treat unverified offering valuation claims as filing facts. |
| 2 | OpenAI containment incident | [OpenAI’s 23 October post](https://openai.com/index/securing-our-frontier-model-training-environment) confirms the 20 September DNS-resolver misconfiguration, limited live-internet access, an 11-day pause, resumption on 1 October, and the offline training approach. | Use OpenAI attribution; omit the reported 2.5-hour duration and any unverified external-lab claim. |
| 3 | Hyperscaler capex | Meta’s [Q3 2026 release](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) reports $34.91B Q3 CapEx including finance-lease principal and a $135–145B 2026 range on that basis. Alphabet’s [Q3 call transcript](https://abc.xyz/investor/events/event-details/2026/2026-Q3-Earnings-Call-2026-Ujou16CTw9/default.aspx) keeps 2026 guidance at $195–205B and reports $51.4B Q3 CapEx, mostly technical infrastructure. Microsoft’s [FY2027 Q1 transcript](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/Investor/events/fy-2027/earnings-fy-2027-q1) estimates about $170B calendar-2026 CapEx due to delayed third-party capacity deliveries; Q1 CapEx was $45.2B, including $7.4B finance leases. Amazon’s official [Q3 call page](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/events/event-details/2026/Q3-2026-Amazoncom-Inc-Earnings-Conference-Call/default.aspx) links to the 29 October webcast audio, but the commonly reported approximately $220B cash-CapEx forecast has only been found in third-party transcripts in this check. | Do not sum these figures as a reconciled AI-only total. Verify Amazon’s audio claim and normalize periods, lease treatment, cash/accrual basis, and non-AI investment first. |
| 4 | Gemini 4 Carbon | [Google DeepMind’s 28 October announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model) confirms staged access through Fairwind and says broader product access will follow without a public date. It lists SEC-bench Pro 88.2%, ExploitGym 54.8%, and CWE-bench 71.0%, explicitly noting external cybersecurity evaluations ran without guardrails; lists introductory API prices of $2/M input and $10/M output tokens. | Attribute performance and price to Google. No independent reproduction or general availability is established by the announcement. Check linked primary documents for current model-card status and Fairwind admission criteria. |
| 5 | GitHub Octoverse | [GitHub’s 2026 report](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-commits-pushes-reach-new-highs-in-2026-as-developers-build-with-agents) confirms over 40% of September pull requests began with an agent’s first draft, up from 17% in March; 110 million in September; and the report’s detection-based scope. It also reports 87% versus 79% multi-file changes and 90% versus about 86% two-week merge rates. | Keep the scope explicitly to GitHub-detected agent involvement; do not generalize to all code or developers. |
| 6 | India AI consultation paper and rules | [News on AIR’s 8 October report](https://newsonair.gov.in/ai-regulation-consultation-paper-to-be-floated-within-a-month-ashwini-vaishnaw/) attributes the “within the next month” expectation to the IT minister. [MediaNama’s 26 October report](https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-india-ai-regulation-consultation-paper-vaishnaw/) says he later described the timetable as “in the coming weeks.” MeitY’s [official FAQ](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/065b6deb585441b5ccdf8be42502a49c.pdf) says G.S.R. 120(E) was notified 10 February and took effect 20 February 2026; defines SGI as realistic synthetic audio, visual, or audio-visual information; describes exclusions including routine edits/accessibility uses; and says the FAQ is explanatory, not a legal instrument. The [consolidated amended rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/550681ab908f8afb135b0ad42816a1c9.pdf) state a three-hour window for the specified removal process following actual knowledge. PIB’s 30 July release also summarizes the amended rules and timeline changes. | Distinguish in-force Gazette rules, MeitY’s explanatory FAQ, and the minister’s attributed timetable statements. Verify the exact operative trigger before describing three-hour removal as a universal deadline. The official paper itself has not been located in this research check. |
| 7 | C-DAC compute tender | C-DAC’s [7 October RFE](https://cdac.in/index.aspx?id=tenders_ASP-RFE) defines AI compute units to include GPUs, accelerators, TPUs, and similar hardware; seeks up to 10,000 units for a five-year service period; permits scaling beyond 10,000 according to demand; and sets Stage 1 empanelment before Stage 2 procurement through GeM. It reserves discretion to allocate work and does not itself show committed deployment. The [27 October corrigendum](https://www.cdac.in/index.aspx?id=tenders_Date-change-corrigendum_13.11.2026) moves submission to 13 November 2026 at 3:30 p.m. and opening to 14 November at 4:00 p.m. | Explain the two-stage procurement boundary and ceiling carefully. Correct the timeline; do not say bids closed on 29 October or opened on 30 October. |
| 8 | EU AI Act dates | [EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2026/1744](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj/eng) is the official text. Its recital 40 confirms the delayed dates for Annex III high-risk systems (2 December 2027) and Annex I product-regulated systems (2 August 2028). The recital on Article 50(2) describes a four-month transition; a source search also surfaced the Commission-hosted implementation timeline stating 2 December 2026, but the operative amending article and final prohibition’s application date have not yet been checked directly. | Keep the two high-risk dates. Before completing the deadline map, cite the relevant operative clauses for Article 50(2), new prohibition, and the regulation’s entry into force. |
| 9 | Workforce announcements | Microsoft’s [26 October official blog post](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/10/26/introducing-a-voluntary-exit-offer/) confirms the voluntary offer but not the press-reported 60,000 eligibility estimate or country list. The [Dutch government’s 26 October statement](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/26/kabinet-werkt-aan-slagvaardige-overheid-eenvoudiger-slimmer-en-kleiner) states an estimated 25,000-FTE reduction over several years, tied to general savings targets; separately, it says AI productivity goals and savings plans are due in Q1 2027. The official statement does not say AI causes the FTE reduction. | Keep the Dutch staffing target distinct from the future AI productivity plan; do not claim AI-caused cuts. Attribute Microsoft’s unconfirmed scope figures to the specific press source or omit them. |
| 10 | Delegated access for AI agents | Primary announcements from [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/1password-for-ai-agents), [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/welcome-to-gemini-at-work-2026), [Chrome](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/chrome/enhanced-autofill-gmail/), and [Opera](https://blogs.opera.com/news/2026/10/webai-local-ai-for-the-web-now-in-opera/) support the product descriptions: brokered sign-in with a human approval step; a Gemini agent receiving its own Workspace account; Chrome autofill suggestions from Gmail; and local models for web use. Bank of America’s reported password warning is not supported by a public primary statement found in this check; the story currently rests on press reporting. | Attribute product behavior to the vendors. Do not imply that local inference, scoped accounts, or brokerage eliminates risk. Omit bank-blocking claims unless supported and clearly attributed. |
