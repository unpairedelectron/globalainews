## Synthetically generated information (SGI)

Audio, visual or audiovisual information meeting the definition in Rule 2(1)(wa) of India’s IT Rules, 2021 as amended in 2026: the material is artificially or algorithmically created, generated, modified or altered using a computer resource so it appears real, authentic or true and depicts a person or event in a way that is, or is likely to be perceived as, indistinguishable from a natural person or real-world event. Check the rule’s conditional exclusions before applying the term.

**Source:** Government of India, G.S.R. 120(E), 10 February 2026, https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/269993.pdf

## Intermediary

For this article, a service provider within the scope of the Information Technology Act and the IT Rules. The exact duty depends on the service’s role and the particular rule; this glossary entry is not a classification of any named company.

**Source:** Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as amended, https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/02/550681ab908f8afb135b0ad42816a1c9.pdf
