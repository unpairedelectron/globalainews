AI models are accelerating the hunt for new antibiotics

A new generation of models is moving beyond prediction — and into the wet lab.

Mira Patel · Science & systems editor · 11 Sep 2026
A new map of the invisible.

The most interesting thing about the latest wave of AI biology models is what they refuse to do. They do not promise a magic button for discovery. Instead, they make the search space legible.

Models can search millions of combinations overnight. Researchers still decide which questions are worth the expensive, messy work of testing.

“The model is not replacing intuition. It is giving intuition somewhere new to look.”

The handoff is the story

That handoff — from suggestion to experiment — is where the field is finding its durable advantage. The interface between models and reality is becoming more precise.
