Fetched: 2026-10-04

## Source and status

Official ISO catalogue page for the published second edition, ISO/IEC 30134-2:2026, dated January 2026.

## Evidence relevant to this explainer

- The standard defines PUE as a data-centre key performance indicator for energy use.
- ISO's public description says the comparison is between energy used by computing equipment and total facility energy consumption.
- The updated edition adds guidance for mixed-use buildings, measurement requirements, unaccounted energy and on-site generation.
- The standard describes measurement categories and rules for calculation and reporting.

## Limits

The catalogue page is a summary, not the full standard. It supports the metric's purpose, current edition and measurement scope, but not a claim that every vendor's reported PUE uses identical boundaries. Comparisons still require the measurement category, period and facility boundary.
