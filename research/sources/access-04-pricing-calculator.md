### Pricing Calculation Guide

To assist you in understanding the cost structure associated with your Bill
of Materials (BOM) derived
from your selected GPU Instances, chosen Pricing Model, and associated
Storage Services, please refer
to the calculation formula provided below:

- Formula for GPU Instances according to various
pricing
models
  - **On-Demand Pricing Model:**


     On-Demand GPU Hourly Pricing × No. of Instances × Required No.
     of Hours selected

  - **One-Month Reserved Pricing Model:**


     One-Month Reserved GPU Hourly Pricing × No. of Instances × 30
     Days × 24 Hours

  - **Six-Months Reserved Pricing Model:**


     Six-Months Reserved GPU Hourly Pricing × No. of Instances × 180
     Days × 24 Hours

  - **Twelve-Months Reserved Pricing Model:**


     Twelve-Months Reserved GPU Hourly Pricing × No. of Instances ×
     365 Days × 24 Hours
- Formula for Storage Services
  - **Block Storage (GB) / Instance:**


     Block Storage Rate per GB per Month × Storage Quantity (GB) ×
     No. of Months

    _(Note: The Number of Months is one for the On-Demand and 1_
    _Month Reserved Models,_
    _six for the 6 Month Reserved Model, and twelve for the 12_
    _Month Reserved Model.)_
  - **Additional Block / File / Object Storage:**


     Block / File / Object Storage Rate per GB per Month × Storage
     Quantity (GB) ×
     Selected No. of Months for Storage
