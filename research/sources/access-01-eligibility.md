##### What is H-index ?



The h-index is a metric used to measure the productivity and citation impact of the
publications of a scientist or scholar. It is based on the set of the researcher's
most cited papers and the number of citations they have received in other
publications.








##### How is the H-Index Calculated ?



**Compile a List of Publications:** Collect all the published papers of
the researcher in question.



**Count Citations:** Determine the number of citations each paper has
received.



**Order Publications:** Arrange the papers in descending order based on
the number of citations.



**Identify the h-index:** The h-index is the highest number ( h ) such
that the researcher has ( h ) papers, each of which has been cited at least ( h )
times.







##### Example:



Suppose a researcher has the following citation counts for their papers:
10, 8, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.




Step 1: Arrange the citation counts in descending order: 10, 8, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1.




Step 2: Find the point where the number of papers (( h )) is equal to or greater than
the number of citations.



Paper 1 has 10 citations



Paper 2 has 8 citations



Paper 3 has 5 citations



Paper 4 has 4 citations



Paper 5 has 3 citations





Here, the h-index is 4 because there are 4 papers with at least 4 citations each. The
next paper has only 3 citations, which is less than 5, so the h-index cannot be 5.

- Publication in ML/AI or allied journals with IF > 2.5, or
- Consolidated 150+ citations

## ![](https://compute.indiaai.gov.in/assets-front/img/Startups.gif) Startups & MSMEs

- DPIIT Recognition







##### What is DPIIT Recognition?



DPIIT Recognition is a certification granted to eligible startups by the Department
for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Government of India.
This recognition is part of the broader Startup India initiative, which aims to
support and nurture startups to drive economic growth and generate employment.







##### Eligibility criteria



**Legal Structure:** Be incorporated as a private limited company, a
registered partnership firm, or a limited liability partnership in India.




**Age of the Entity:** Be a new entity not older than 10 years from the
date of incorporation.




**Annual Turnover:** Have an annual turnover not exceeding INR 100 crore
in any of the previous financial years.








##### Application process



**Online Application:** Startups can apply for recognition through the
Startup India portal by providing details about their business, team, product or
service, and funding.




**Documentation:** Required documents include a certificate of
incorporation or registration, a description of the business and how it qualifies as a
startup, and any other relevant documents supporting the innovation claim.



**Review and Approval:** Once the application is submitted, it is
reviewed by the DPIIT. If the startup meets all the criteria, a certificate of
recognition is typically issued within two working days.

- Experience in AI/ML
- Annual rev 50 Lacs or Funding of INR 100 Lacs
