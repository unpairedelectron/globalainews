# AIKosh platform Terms of Use

**Capture date:** 4 October 2026  
**Source:** https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/terms-n-conditions

The Terms of Use identify AIKosh as a platform designed, developed, and hosted by the IndiaAI Division under MeitY. They describe datasets, models, tools, and libraries as user-submitted artifacts and state that dataset reuse is subject to the specified licenses and end-use terms.

This is platform-level language, not a determination of the permissions for any individual record. For reuse, consult the item page, linked terms, and source owner's conditions.
