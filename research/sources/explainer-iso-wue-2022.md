Title: ISO/IEC 30134-9:2022 — Water usage effectiveness (WUE)
Fetched: 2026-10-04

## Source and status

Official ISO catalogue page for the published first edition, ISO/IEC 30134-9:2022, dated March 2022.

## Evidence relevant to this explainer

- The standard defines WUE as a KPI for quantifying data-centre water consumption during the use phase.
- It introduces WUE measurement categories and describes their relationship to data-centre infrastructure, IT equipment and IT operations.
- It specifies measurement, calculation, reporting and interpretation requirements.

## Limits

The public catalogue abstract does not expose the complete calculation rules. Do not treat all values called “WUE” as directly comparable without identifying their category, numerator, denominator, period and reporting boundary.
