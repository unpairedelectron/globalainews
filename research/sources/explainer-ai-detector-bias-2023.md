Title: GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers — Patterns
Published: 2023
Fetched: 2026-10-04

## Evidence relevant to this explainer

The peer-reviewed study evaluated seven AI-text detectors on 91 TOEFL essays written by non-native English speakers and 88 essays by U.S. eighth-grade students. It reports an average false-positive rate of 61.3% on the TOEFL essays; all detectors unanimously flagged 19.8%, and at least one detector flagged 97.8%.

## Limits

These results concern the detector versions and two essay sets evaluated in this study. They are not an estimate of the false-positive rate for every detector, writing genre, language group or current 2026 product. The study supports caution about treating classifier output as proof of authorship.
