# PIB: nearly 38,000 GPUs onboarded

Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2291450&lang=1&reg=3  
Title: “Innovation, Startups, MSMEs and Quality Manufacturing are Pillars of India's Future Growth”  
Publisher: Press Information Bureau, Ministry of Commerce and Industry  
Published: 2026-07-29  
Fetched: 2026-10-04

## Relevant primary evidence

The release says that under the IndiaAI Mission, “nearly 38,000 GPUs have already been onboarded” to provide affordable computing capacity to startups and researchers, with further expansion planned.

## Scope and citation use

This is a dated primary restatement of the programme’s “onboarded” label after the 25 March PIB figure of 38,231. It gives no new exact count, provider breakdown, status definition, or crosswalk to the PIB factsheet’s 45,000+ “shared capacity” claim as of June 2026. Preserve the rounded wording and do not interpret it as installed, free, or schedulable supply.
