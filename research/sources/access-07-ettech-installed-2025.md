## Relevant secondary reporting

ET reported that a little over 17,300 GPUs had been successfully installed under the IndiaAI Mission tender. It attributed the figure to people aware of developments and a presentation at a 16 June 2025 review meeting with empanelled cloud service providers. The same article said providers still had installation and integration work underway, with a deadline of 7 August 2025.

## Scope and citation use

This is an attributed, secondary installation-stage report, not a dated official inventory. It describes an earlier rollout and cannot establish how many GPUs were installed, connected, reserved, or schedulable later. Keep it distinct from the mission target, empanelled and onboarded figures, and current access claims.
