# AIKosh dataset-listing sample

**Capture date:** 4 October 2026  
**Method:** Five public AIKosh detail pages were inspected as displayed on the capture date. The sample was purposively selected to include differing license, visibility, source and hosting fields. It is not random or representative of the full catalog. The review covers listing metadata only; it does not download, test, or legally evaluate any dataset.

## Reading cautions

`NA` is recorded as the listing's displayed value; it is not interpreted here as a particular legal status. An `Open` visibility label is an access field, not a substitute for the artifact's license or end-use terms. The AIKosh quality score is explicitly marked beta and is treated as platform-displayed metadata, not an independent audit of the underlying files. Listing contents and values may change after capture.
