Title: Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE): A Green Grid Data Center Sustainability Metric
Fetched: 2026-10-04

## Evidence relevant to this explainer

The 2011 Green Grid paper describes a site-based WUE ratio using annual site water usage divided by IT equipment energy. It also describes a source-based measure that adds water associated with producing the site's energy. Both use IT equipment energy as the denominator.

## Limits

This is a historical methodology paper, not the current ISO standard. Use it to explain why a WUE number needs a stated measurement category; use ISO/IEC 30134-9:2022 for the current standard's scope and reporting framework. Do not assume that every operator's published “WUE” uses the same category.
