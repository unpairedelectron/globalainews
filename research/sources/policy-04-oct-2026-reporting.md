# IndiaAI Mission likely to rewire after GPU glitch

![IndiaAI Mission likely to rewire after GPU glitch](https://img.etimg.com/thumb/height-450,width-600,imgsize-363781,msid-134600390/tech/technology/indiaai-mission-likely-to-rewire-after-gpu-glitch.jpg)

### Synopsis

## The IndiaAI Mission currently has access to about 30,000 GPUs against a total commitment of 45,000. Several firms that had committed capacity have been unable to deliver due to market pressures. The IT ministry is now planning to issue fresh bids while also pressing companies to meet their pledges.

By [Suraksha P](https://m.economictimes.com/etreporter/author-suraksha-p-479244511.cms) & [Surabhi Agarwal](https://m.economictimes.com/etreporter/author-surabhi-agarwal-479241991.cms), ETtech

8 mins read, Oct 1, 2026, 06:00:00 AM IST

The government is planning to recalibrate the IndiaAI Mission as the rollout of critical compute components, particularly GPUs, faces delays and mounting cost pressures. That follows a sharp rise in memory and GPU prices since the programme was launched in March 2024. In most instances, prices have more than doubled over the past year.
