## Relevant evidence

The study tests language models on multi-document question answering and key-value retrieval while changing where the answer-bearing information appears in the input. It reports that performance is often strongest when relevant information is near the beginning or end and can fall when the same information is placed in the middle of a long context.

The paper’s central contribution is a controlled evaluation protocol that varies context length and evidence position. Its results describe the models tested at the time, not current 2026 products.

## Citation scope

Use this work to motivate position-aware long-context testing. Do not present it as a direct performance result for current commercial models.
