## Evidence relevant to this explainer

The paper proposes model cards as documentation accompanying trained models. It recommends disclosing intended use cases, performance across conditions relevant to application domains, evaluation procedures and other context that helps users judge suitability.

## Limits

The paper proposes a reporting framework; a model card is not an independent audit, safety certification or guarantee that all material limitations are disclosed. Current provider cards vary in structure and depth.
