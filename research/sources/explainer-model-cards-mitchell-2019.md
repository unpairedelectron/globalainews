## Evidence relevant to this explainer

The ACM abstract proposes model cards as short documents accompanying trained models. It recommends reporting intended-use cases, performance across conditions relevant to application domains, evaluation procedures, and other information that helps users judge suitability.

## Limits

The paper proposes a reporting framework; it is not an independent audit, safety certification, or guarantee that all material limitations are disclosed. Current provider cards vary in structure and depth.
