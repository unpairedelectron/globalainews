# How to Apply forCompute ?

This feature provides end users with the capability to apply for
compute resources through the India AI Compute Portal. It includes
functionalities that verify the submission of mandatory documents
and confirm the user's eligibility before allowing access to the
requested compute resources.


### Login with NSSO

Login to the IndiaAI Compute Portal using Digilocker, e-Pramaan or Jan Parichay

### User Verification

Details submitted would be verified and approved. Post verification you can apply for compute
services

### Compute Request Approval

Requests would be approved by PMEC as per SLAs.


![apply](https://www.compute.indiaai.gov.in/assets-front/img/how-apply.png)

### End User Registration

Complete your profile by providing the required information and documents and submitting the form for
end user registration.

### Apply for Compute

Submit a request for compute services required with a draft Bill based on the rate list. This
includes a request for subsidy.

# FAQs

The IndiaAI Mission aims to bolster
India’s global leadership in AI, foster technological
self-reliance, ensure ethical and responsible AI deployment, and
democratize the benefits of AI across all strata of society.


### What are the AI services offered by IndiaAI on the cloud?

IndiaAI offers AI compute instances, network services, storage services, and various AI platforms
and other services, including MLOps, LLMOps, and AI services for translation, transliteration,
OCR, and audio processing.

### Who is eligible to use IndiaAI empaneled AI services?

Eligibility extends to researchers, academic faculties, IndiaAI fellowship awardees, students,
startups, MSMEs, and government entities, each with specific criteria and supporting documents.
Detailed eligibility criteria may be referred under the [Eligibility Criteria](https://www.compute.indiaai.gov.in/login#values)
tab


### What documents are required for registration?

Required documents vary by user category. Please refer to the detailed [eligibility\\
criteria](https://www.compute.indiaai.gov.in/login#values)

### How do I register for access to AI services?

Users must register on the IndiaAI compute portal using Meri Pehchaan using their Digilocker,
Parichay or ePramaan account. Next users would be expected to fill a registration form and upload
relevant documents to establish their eligibility. The completed registration forms would be
verified by designated verifying officials. Once the verifier approvers, user can submit request
for accessing compute infrastructure.

### What is the process for accessing compute?

Post successful registration, users must submit a detailed project proposal, including technical
approach, envisioned impact, and estimated bill of materials. In case the requested GPU hours is
less than 5000, the compute request is auto approved. For greater than 5000 GPU hours, approval
would be provided by the PMEC committee. Requests should be submitted between 1-25th of the month.
PMEC would publish the final list of approved users on the 10th of next month or next working day.


### How is GPU allocation determined?

GPU allocation is based on user eligibility, the number of GPU hours requested, and whether a
subsidy is sought. Requests are reviewed by the PMEC if they exceed certain thresholds.

### What are the payment terms for using AI services?

IndiaAI subsidizes approved projects, but users must bear additional costs beyond the subsidy.
Payments are made to the service provider as per their billing cycle.

### What is the validity period for service approvals?

Approvals are valid for 30 calendar days, and users must start using the services within this
period to avoid expiration.


### How does IndiaAI ensure the security and proper use of AI services?

Security measures include IP address whitelisting, multi-factor authentication, role-based access
controls, and regular audits.

### What should I do if I encounter issues with service availability?

Empanelled agencies are required to ensure accessibility as per defined SLAs. Users may reach out
to their service provided as per the support details published by them
