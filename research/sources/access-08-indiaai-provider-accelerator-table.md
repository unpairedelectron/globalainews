## Relevant primary evidence

The IndiaAI-hosted PDF contains 14 numbered bidder or consortium rows and a `TOTAL` row of 45,081. Its columns list NVIDIA and AMD GPU models alongside Intel Gaudi, AWS Trainium and Inferentia, and Google Trillium accelerator categories. The NVIDIA and AMD model columns sum to 38,085; the Intel, AWS, and Google columns sum to 6,996. These are arithmetic sums of the table's displayed columns, not a source-defined GPU-only denominator.

The file metadata records 2 September 2026 as the PDF creation and modification date. The document itself has no publication or inventory-as-of date, and it does not define what each count represents (for example, offered, empanelled, onboarded, installed, active, reserved, or schedulable units). Its filename and the portal context associate the rows with empanelled providers, but the PDF does not define that status or connect its total to an operational state.

## Scope and citation use

Use this as a dated, official provider-and-accelerator-category listing only. Do not treat the 45,081 total as a count of GPUs alone, installed hardware, live supply, or currently schedulable capacity. It does not provide a valid crosswalk to the 34,333 empanelled-plus-additional figure, 38,231 or “nearly 38,000” onboarded figures, 45,000+ shared-capacity figure, or ETtech's report of about 30,000 accessible GPUs.
